The graph above shows the presence of files for the day selected. Please note that the 24 hour period is in GMT/UTC time, not local time. There are occasional gaps and missing data for each site in the archive. These gaps are caused by a number of reasons, including scheduled maintenance at the Radar sites, unplanned downtime due to severe weather, communications problems or archival problems. Most locations have coverage from multiple Radar sites and the next closest site may provide sufficient information during data gaps. The NCEI Interactive Map Tool includes site locations and coverage maps.

Due to the size of the NEXRAD Archive, instantaneous access is not possible. The user is emailed when the ordered data has posted to the NCEI order download site. Orders typically take between 5 and 25 minutes to complete.

Orders from this system are limited to a single day and site. Download data for multiple days and from multiple sites (up to 250 GB per order) from the NCEI Climate Data Online (CDO) System or NCEI Archive Access System.
